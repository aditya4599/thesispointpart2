How a report gets written

Four steps sit behind every Reco we publish.

  1. 1

    Start from the filings

    Every figure comes from the company’s own annual reports and filings, not a data aggregator.

  2. 2

    Build the model ourselves

    Three linked statements and our own forecasts, so you can see which assumption moves the answer.

  3. 3

    Value it the way it earns

    Discounted cash flow for growth companies, excess returns for banks and lenders, normalised multiples for cyclicals.

  4. 4

    Say what would prove us wrong

    Each report names the risks to the thesis and what would make us change the rating.
